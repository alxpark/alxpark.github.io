    Chip & file

    Chip core is a C port of MAME's k054539 (8-bit PCM, 16-bit PCM, 4-bit DPCM, reverse playback, loop points, 16 KB echo RAM, key-on latching), compiled to a 12 KB WebAssembly module. Native rate is clock ÷ 384 (48 kHz at 18.432 MHz), resampled to your audio device.

    Only K054539 writes are played. Other chips in a VGM, such as a YM2151 in Xexex, are listed above but stay silent. Find K054539 sets on vgmrips.net (Konami GX, Mystic Warriors and related boards). Packs come as .zip files: open or drop the whole pack to get a playlist.

    Load URL needs the file's server to allow cross-site downloads (CORS). Files on raw.githubusercontent.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net work directly; other sites are retried through the CORS proxy (corsproxy.io by default). Add ?url=<link> to this page's address to open a file on start.
