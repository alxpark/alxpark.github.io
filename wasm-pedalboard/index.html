SQ-16

Mono step sequencer · A minor pentatonic

Tap an empty cell to place a note, tap it again to accent it, a third time to clear.

Signal path, per sample in WASM: input × gain + synth → Grit (tanh-style clipper with bias, one-pole tone, DC blocker) → Shift (two crossfaded delay taps sweeping a 70 ms window) → Hall (Freeverb: 8 combs + 4 allpasses per side, 23-sample stereo spread) → soft limiter.

Bypass crossfades over ~15 ms instead of hard switching, so stomping never clicks. Reverb tails ring out when you bypass Hall.
