The engine searches a 64-bit bitboard with negamax, alpha-beta pruning, a 4M-entry transposition table and iterative deepening. On Strong and Solver it also tries to prove the exact result with a null-window search to the end of the game; once it proves a win it will not let go. Press 1–7 to drop a disc.
