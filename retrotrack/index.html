Songs are standard ProTracker MODs (M.K. for 4 channels, 6CHN/8CHN otherwise) and open in any tracker. Downloads arrive zipped. Your work autosaves in this browser. You can also drop a .mod or an audio file onto the page.

Keys

Z S X D C V G B H N J M play the lower octave, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U I the upper one. In Edit mode they write notes with the current sample.

0–9 A–F type into the sample and effect columns; a finished field drops to the next row. Space toggles Edit, Enter plays the song, Shift+Enter the pattern, Esc stops. F4 cycles the Editor, Visual and Scopes views. Ctrl+O or the Web… button loads a song from The Mod Archive, Modland or AMP.

Arrows move, Tab jumps channels, PgUp/PgDn move 16 rows, Del clears, Ins/Backspace insert or pull rows. F1/F2 pick the octave, Ctrl+←/→ the sample.

Shift+arrows or drag select a block. Ctrl+C X V copy, cut, paste. Ctrl+↑/↓ transpose a semitone (add Shift for an octave). Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

Tap a channel header to mute it, long-press or right-click to solo.

How it sounds like an Amiga

The replayer is a C port of ProTracker 2.3D's logic compiled to WebAssembly, running in an AudioWorklet. It keeps PT's quirks: the doubled 9xx offset, the tremolo ramp bug, BPM changes landing one tick late, and arpeggio reads past the period table.

Each channel is a Paula voice: it fetches sample words by DMA, latches period changes only between sample points and swaps in loop pointers when its length counter runs out, so sample swapping works as on hardware. Output goes through band-limited steps and the A500 or A1200 RC filters. 6 and 8 channel songs mix extra voices the same way.

E8x (Karplus-Strong) is ignored, as in pt2-clone's default.
