Lava fuses sand into glass. Water quenches lava into stone and turns fire to steam. Plants drink water and spread through it, then burn. Oil floats and goes up fast. Acid eats through sand, stone, plants and oil, but not glass or walls. Gunpowder explodes on contact with fire or lava and sets off any powder nearby. Steam rises, cools and rains back down. Right-click or pick Erase to remove cells.
